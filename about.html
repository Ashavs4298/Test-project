About artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a field of computing that builds systems able to perform tasks that often call for human abilities, such as recognizing patterns, understanding language, and making predictions.

How AI learns

Many AI systems learn from examples. A model finds patterns in training data and uses them to make a prediction or generate a response. People design, test, and guide these systems, and the quality of their results depends on the data and instructions they receive.

Using AI thoughtfully

AI can help people explore ideas, summarize information, and handle routine tasks. It can also make mistakes or reflect gaps in its training data, so important answers should be checked and personal information should be handled with care.
